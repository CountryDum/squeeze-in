Our story

A Grant County table where the coffee keeps coming and nobody leaves hungry.

The Squeeze-In has been feeding locals, travelers and out-of-town guests for decades. After many years on Main Street in John Day, we moved up the road to Canyon City, a historic gold strike town, and brought the whole menu with us.

The daily fare is always filling, satisfying and tasty, and the ticket won't cost you your next paycheck. Come in, relax and enjoy a good meal.
